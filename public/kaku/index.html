Find Your AI Transition Stage

Most people are not lost. They simply have not named their stage yet.

01

Observe

You are noticing AI everywhere, but have not connected it to your own work.

02

Explore

You are trying tools, prompts, and examples, but still lack a clear practice system.

03

Practice

You are applying AI to real tasks and building confidence through repetition.

04

Navigate

You can decide where AI helps, where it misleads, and how to move with uncertainty.

05

Design

You redesign workflows, learning paths, and team systems around AI-native thinking.

06

Guide

You help others make sense of the shift and move from anxiety into action.

07

Catalyze

You create momentum, contribution, and new learning loops for the community.
