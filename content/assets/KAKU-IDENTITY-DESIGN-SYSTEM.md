# KA-KU Identity Design System

The homepage hero is the master reference for the KA-KU Identity visual language. Reuse `app/components/kaku-identity-hero.tsx` and the `--identity-*` design tokens rather than rebuilding its effects per page.

## Three usage levels

1. **Full identity hero** — homepage, major campaigns and flagship movement pages.
2. **Identity section** — use selected protocol, orbit, pixel or scan motifs in one major interior section.
3. **Identity accent** — cyan/pink borders, monospace labels and offset shadows for cards, tags and buttons.

## Core visual DNA

- Black foundation, cyan signal, pink disruption, lime momentum and community purple.
- Oversized editorial headline with monospace protocol metadata.
- Pixelated KA-KU identity image, fine grid, scan lines, RGB displacement and orbital curves.
- Square CTA with cyan border and offset pink shadow.
- Motion respects reduced-motion preferences.

## Shorthand for future requests

> Use the KA-KU Identity system at full, section or accent level.

This keeps visual intensity appropriate while preserving one consistent brand language.
