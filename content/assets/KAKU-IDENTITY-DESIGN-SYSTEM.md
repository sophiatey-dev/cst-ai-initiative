# KA-KU Identity Design System

The homepage hero is the master reference for the KA-KU Identity visual
language. Reuse the component and tokens instead of recreating the effect on
each page.

## Reusable component

- Component: `app/components/kaku-identity-hero.tsx`
- Purpose: major landing-page heroes, campaign openings and flagship
  announcements.
- Page-specific content is supplied through props. The protocol background,
  scanning layer, glitch lines, identity circle, typography hierarchy and CTA
  pattern remain centralized.

## Core visual DNA

- Deep black base: `--identity-bg`
- Cyber cyan: `--identity-cyan`
- Signal pink: `--identity-pink`
- Momentum lime: `--identity-lime`
- Community purple: `--identity-purple`
- Editorial oversized uppercase headline
- Monospace protocol labels and metadata
- Pixelated KA-KU identity image
- Fine grid, scan lines, RGB displacement and orbital curves
- Square high-contrast CTA with an offset pink shadow
- Motion must stop when the visitor prefers reduced motion

## Usage levels

### Full identity hero

Use for the homepage, major CST AI Initiative campaigns and Ka-Ku movement
pages. It includes the full image, protocol treatment and primary CTA pair.

### Identity section

Use one or two motifs—protocol label, cyan border, pixelated image or circular
orbit—for a major interior section. Do not repeat the complete hero treatment
several times on the same page.

### Identity accent

Use cyan/pink borders, monospace labels and the offset shadow for cards,
calendar highlights, speaker tags or campaign buttons.

## Content rules

- Headline: two short declarative lines; highlight one phrase in lime or pink.
- Eyebrow: initiative, programme or community name.
- Description: one clear human-centred promise, ideally under 35 words.
- Primary CTA: the immediate action.
- Secondary CTA: exploration or context.
- Protocol label: short system-like status text; never replace meaningful copy.

## Do not

- Add rainbow gradients or unrelated neon colours.
- Use glitch effects on body copy.
- animate every element simultaneously.
- use the identity treatment for ordinary informational sections.
- substitute the KA-KU identity image with an unrelated cyberpunk character.

## Future instruction shorthand

When requesting a new section, say:

> Use the KA-KU Identity system at full, section or accent level.

This tells the builder which visual intensity to apply while preserving one
consistent brand language.

## CST AI Initiative page application

- Home: full identity hero and high-energy ecosystem storytelling.
- Forum, Workshops, Membership and Community: identity-section intensity with
  orbital hero fields, protocol rails and stronger card signals.
- Calendar, AI Pulse, Enterprise and About: identity accents with the same
  metadata, grid, borders and navigation language.
- Header, footer, buttons, tags, imagery labels and motion rules remain shared
  across every route.
