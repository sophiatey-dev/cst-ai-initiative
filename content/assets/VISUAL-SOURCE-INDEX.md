## Selection priority

1. Use authentic Sophia and CST material when the section makes a factual claim
   about the founder, CST history, an event, a workshop or the community.
2. Use Summit material to communicate public credibility, scale, leadership and
   community participation.
3. Use Camp material to communicate learning, transformation, experimentation
   and close human connection.
4. Use AI-generated or licensed placeholder visuals only when no suitable
   authentic asset exists.
5. For website use, select the strongest individual file and place an optimized
   copy under `public/assets/`; do not embed Drive preview URLs directly.

## Asset-handling rules

- Preserve the original in Google Drive; place only optimized web copies in the
  website repository.
- Prefer landscape images at 2400px or wider for heroes and portrait images at
  1200px or wider for profiles.
- Prefer MP4/WebM for website video. Keep hero loops short, muted and compressed.
- Record the source folder and event name in the filename or accompanying note.
- Confirm usage permission before publishing identifiable participants,
  testimonials or private-event footage.
- Avoid presenting AI-generated concept imagery as a documentary photograph;
  label it clearly when applicable.
